# Smol Community DIY

Explore community-created builds, straps, and docks to help you assemble or customize your own Smol trackers.

## What you'll find in this section

- **[Smol Community Builds](../hardware/smol-slimes-community-builds.md)**
  Showcases community-submitted Smol tracker builds, including photos and build notes.

- **[Smol Strap Designs](../hardware/smol-slimes-strap-designs.md)**
  Community-sourced designs and guides for creating comfortable and secure straps for Smol trackers.

- **[Smol Community Docks](./smol-slimes-community-docks.md)**
  Community-sourced designs and guides for docks compatible with Smol trackers.
